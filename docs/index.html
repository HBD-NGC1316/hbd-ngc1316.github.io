Ph.D. Student in Computer Science · RPI

Hyejin Park

Grounded & Trustworthy AI · Multimodal Concept Learning

My research asks how intelligent systems can form reliable concepts from language, vision, and interaction—especially in unfamiliar situations.

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute, advised by Prof. Tomek Strzalkowski. I am affiliated with the Language and Cognition Artificial Intelligence Lab and the Tetherless World Constellation.

Before joining RPI, I worked on knowledge retrieval, recommendation systems, and applied AI research in South Korea.

Current Research Focus

I study grounded and trustworthy AI, focusing on how intelligent systems form and refine concepts from language, vision, and interaction. My current work examines multimodal concept learning and object–action understanding, especially how models interpret unfamiliar situations without relying only on familiar labels or conventional object functions.
